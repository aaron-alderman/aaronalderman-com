# Worldwright storytelling exposition

**Date:** 5 November 2026  
**Status:** evolving product exposition and experience proposal  
**Stage:** symmetry exposition retained as the design record; first prototype execution authorized

Worldwright is being explored as a storytelling product: an author composes a world that readers can find their own way through. Aaron's concepts, work and lived experience supply the first world. The personal expedition is the first storytelling composition being developed within it.

The exposition exposes the shared structure, meaningful differences and possible compositions. The documentation records proposals and the direction expressed in the originating conversation; it does not establish product demand or change the status of cited research. Aaron subsequently authorized execution on 5 November 2026. The first implementation is the [Worldwright expedition prototype](../../../applications/worldwright-expedition/README.md); the compositions remain open to revision through review.

## Product and first example

| Layer | Working meaning | First example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | The environment for authoring and presenting storytelling worlds and compositions. | Worldwright. |
| World | People, concepts, events, places, artifacts and relationships available to tell with. | Aaron's intellectual and personal world. |
| Composition | Authored rules and arrangements governing how encounters form a story. | The personal expedition. |
| Journey | A reader's particular traversal and encounters. | Observation, holonomy, personal experience, care and accountable systems. |
| Presentation | The visual, verbal, spatial and interaction language of a composition. | The Monster Hunt expedition treatment. |

The working product sentence is: **Worldwright lets you compose stories people find their own way through.** This wording is a proposal.

## Origin and source posture

The initiating brief developed from a proposed renewal of aaronalderman.com into a tour of Aaron's brain: concepts encountered through a choose-your-own-adventure experience. Aaron then supplied the emotional connection to family separation, marital loss, kenosis, trust, care and the danger of totalizing intensity. He identified the [Halloween Monster Hunt](https://aaron-alderman.github.io/halloween-monster-hunt/) as the meta-theme, suggested proceeding repository by repository, and identified Worldwright as the storytelling product behind this particular composition.

The conceptual passages and screen copy are assistant-authored proposals based on that conversation and the linked corpus. Personal material remains Aaron's attributed account; wording and publication treatment require further authorship. No independent account of other family members is supplied here.

The existing [Worldwright research landscape](../worldwright-research/current_landscape_2026-10-23.md) concerns a synthetic intervention model. Its replay and counterfactual disciplines may inform a future scenario composition. Its simulation results do not validate this storytelling product, and these notes do not rename or replace that programme.
